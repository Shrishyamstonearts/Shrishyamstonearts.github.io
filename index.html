About Our Legacy

Welcome to Jay Shri Shyam Stone Arts, based in Lohardaga (Ranchi, Jharkhand). Led by Dinesh Saini (CEO) and Sameer Saini (Director), we specialize in high-end natural stone architecture, turning houses into regal structures and crafting divine temple spaces with over 15+ years of dedicated artistry.

Our Premium Stone Materials

  • Bansi Paharpur Sandstone: Premium royal pink sandstone for grand temples & heritage luxury facades.
  • Jodhpur Pink Sandstone: Highly durable, uniform texture stone perfect for carved pillars & elevations.
  • Dholpur Sandstone (Beige/Pink/Red): Weather-proof natural stone ideal for fine jali work & wall cladding.
  • All-India Natural Stones: Custom work available in Marble, Sandstone, and Granite sourced from top quarries across India.

Our Core Specializations

Temple Architecture

Complete stone temple construction including Shikhar, Sabha Mandap, Garbhagriha, and hand-carved deity wall panels.

House Exterior Elevations

Royal Rajasthani & modern natural stone cladding for luxury bungalows, villas, and commercial buildings.

Carved Pillars & Balconies

Custom-carved structural columns, Jharokhas, decorative stone brackets, and balcony railings.

Custom Stone Jali Work

Intricate geometric and floral precision-cut stone jali panels for ventilation, privacy, and aesthetic elegance.
